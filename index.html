About me

I started with HTML and CSS and fell in love with turning an idea into something people can open on their phone. Every project since has taught me something new about how the web really works.

I'm studying Business Administration and Management, and it gives me a different lens on development. Before I write a line of code, I ask who the website is for, what it needs to achieve, and whether it will earn its place for the person who owns it.

Today I'm growing across the full stack with JavaScript, React, Node.js, and MongoDB, so I can take a product from first sketch to working database.

I'm open to remote and international opportunities, freelance and full-time, on frontend or backend work. If you have something in mind, get in touch.
